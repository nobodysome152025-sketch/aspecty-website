Brand identity

Logo Usage Rules

These rules help maintain a consistent and professional ASPECTY brand identity.

✓ Correct Usage

  • Use the original logo artwork whenever available.
  • Keep the logo proportions unchanged.
  • Use a clear background with sufficient contrast.
  • Leave enough space around the logo.
  • Use approved versions for light and dark backgrounds.

✕ Incorrect Usage

  • Do not stretch, squash or distort the logo.
  • Do not change its colours without approval.
  • Do not add unapproved shadows or effects.
  • Do not place it on a confusing background.
  • Do not claim ASPECTY branding as your own.
Logo ownership: ASPECTY's original branding remains the property of its respective owner. Clients receive rights to commissioned logos according to their agreed project terms.
